Glossary of Terms
Kp Index
A 0-9 scale measuring geomagnetic activity caused by solar wind. Higher Kp = stronger aurora. In Iceland (latitude ~64-66), Kp 2+ is often visible to the naked eye. Kp 5+ is a geomagnetic storm with vivid, dancing aurora.
Clear Sky / % Clear
The percentage of sky free from clouds. 100% clear = no clouds at all. You need mostly clear skies (70%+) to see the aurora. Even strong aurora is invisible behind clouds.
Darkness
Aurora is only visible when the sky is dark — after astronomical twilight (sun more than 12 degrees below the horizon). In Iceland, winter nights are long and ideal; summer has midnight sun with no aurora viewing.
Light Pollution
Artificial light from towns and cities that washes out the night sky. This app finds spots far from populated areas so the sky is as dark as possible. Labels: "Very dark" (best), "Dark" (good), "Moderate" (OK).
Flat Terrain
Flat, open land gives you a wide view of the sky in all directions, so you can see aurora even on the horizon. The app checks elevation data to find spots without mountains or hills blocking your view.
Elevation
Height above sea level in meters. Shown to help you understand the terrain. Higher spots aren't necessarily better — flat and dark matters more than altitude for aurora viewing.
Score Badges
Great = excellent conditions, go now. Good = solid chance of seeing aurora. Fair = possible but conditions are marginal. Not visible = impossible right now (daytime or total cloud cover).
